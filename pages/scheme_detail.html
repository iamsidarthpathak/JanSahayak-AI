PM Scholarship Scheme

Status: Eligible ✅

The PM Scholarship Scheme provides financial assistance to eligible students pursuing higher education in recognized institutions across India.

Benefits

  • Up to ₹50,000 scholarship per year
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Financial support for higher education
  • Support for tuition and academic expenses

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be an Indian citizen
  • Must have passed Class 12 or equivalent
  • Must meet the prescribed family income criteria
  • Must satisfy the scheme-specific eligibility requirements

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Educational Certificates / Mark Sheets
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Parent's Aadhaar card

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official scheme website.
  2. Register using your mobile number and email.
  3. Fill in the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Review your application and submit it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for this scheme?

Students who meet the eligibility criteria can apply.

How is the scholarship amount transferred?

The amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account.
